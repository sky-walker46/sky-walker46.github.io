Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Instagram Reel Automation is a personal software project developed by Aditya Ranjan. It helps organize authorized video content and manage workflows involving Instagram, Google Drive, and YouTube.

1. Information accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the application uses Google and YouTube APIs to access the channel identity, video information, upload functionality, and playlist functionality needed for its workflows.

2. How information is used

Authorized information is used to identify the selected YouTube channel, upload videos, manage playlists, track upload progress, and prevent accidental duplicate uploads.

3. Data storage

OAuth authorization tokens and upload tracking records are stored locally on the operator's computer. The application may also store authorized video files in Google Drive and publish selected videos to YouTube.

Local tracking records may include Instagram source identifiers, Reel numbers, YouTube video IDs, upload statuses, and playlist information.

4. Data sharing

Data is transmitted to Google services, including Google Drive and YouTube, when required to perform authorized operations. The application is not designed to sell personal information or share OAuth credentials with advertisers.

5. Data retention and deletion

The operator retains local credentials, tracking records, and source videos until they are manually removed. The application does not automatically delete original video files.

The operator can delete locally stored credentials and tracking records, remove files from Google Drive, and manage uploaded videos through YouTube Studio. Revoking authorization does not automatically delete videos already published on YouTube.

6. Access and authorization

Google account access is granted through Google's OAuth authorization process. The account holder can revoke access at any time through Google Account Permissions .

7. Third-party services

This application uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service . Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy .

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the automation's functionality or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
