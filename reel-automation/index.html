Personal Automation Project

A simpler way to manage video workflows.

Instagram Reel Automation is a personal software project designed to help organize authorized short-form video content across Instagram, Google Drive, and YouTube.

What the application does

  • Organizes Instagram Reel links and downloaded videos.
  • Uploads and verifies video files stored in Google Drive.
  • Helps manage YouTube video uploads and playlists.
  • Tracks upload progress to avoid accidental duplicates.

Google and YouTube integration

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access permitted YouTube account functionality. Access is granted by the account holder through Google's consent process.

The application is intended for personal use with content and accounts that the operator owns or is authorized to manage.
